Vendor risk assessment is an essential component of third-party risk management, which focuses on identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks that may arise from an organization’s relationships with third-party vendors and suppliers. Digital Defense Group is delivering reliable vendor risk assessment services to help organizations mitigate the potential threats that third-party vendors might pose to their operations.

Effective vendor risk assessment requires a risk-based approach that involves identifying and characterizing each vendor’s potential risk to the organization based on its product or service offering, regulatory landscape, geographical location and reputation.

The assessment process involves reviewing critical elements such as information security resilience, business continuity plans, data protection policies, and compliance with relevant legal and regulatory frameworks.

DDG can help organizations perform vendor risk assessments by developing questionnaires and assessing existing and potential vendors against appropriate criteria. we can also provide support in creating an effective vendor risk management program by designing policies and procedures that address the organization’s risk management needs.
